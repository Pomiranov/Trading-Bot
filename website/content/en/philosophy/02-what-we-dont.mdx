Confidence is bounded to the 0.05–0.95 range. No strategy starts at full trust, and none reaches it. A signal below the 0.20 floor is recorded but not executed. Position size comes from volatility and a fixed risk budget: confidence decides whether a trade happens, it does not yet scale how large it is.
