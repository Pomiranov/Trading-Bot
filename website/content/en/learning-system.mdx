Every closed trade updates confidence in the strategy that made it: not the single outcome, but an equal-weighted mean of win rate, profit factor and expectancy. Below 20 trades the number barely moves. Above it, it eases toward the target at 0.15 per update, and never reaches certainty in either direction.
